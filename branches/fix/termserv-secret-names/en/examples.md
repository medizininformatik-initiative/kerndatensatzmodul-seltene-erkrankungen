## Examples

This area contains comprehensive case examples for the practical application of the FHIR profiles of the Rare Diseases module. The examples demonstrate semantic annotation and the correct use of the profiles based on realistic clinical scenarios.

### Available case examples

#### Marfan syndrome

A detailed example of a genetically determined connective tissue disease with multi-system involvement. The example shows:

* Clinical and genetic diagnosis
* HPO-based phenotyping
* Family history with autosomal-dominant inheritance
* Therapy management and follow-up

#### Spinal muscular atrophy (SMA)

A case example of a neuromuscular disease with different degrees of severity. The example illustrates:

* Early diagnosis through newborn screening
* Genetic confirmation and subtyping
* Gene therapy documentation
* Course monitoring and function preservation

### Purpose of the examples

These case examples serve as:

* **Implementation aid**: concrete application of the abstract profiles
* **Validation**: verification of profile conformance
* **Training material**: understanding of the data modeling
* **Test data**: basis for interoperability tests

The examples use realistic but anonymized data and cover typical use cases in the field of rare diseases.
